Frequently Asked Questions

What is your shipping policy?

We ship worldwide! Shipping costs and times vary by destination. You can find detailed information on our Shopier store.

Do you accept returns?

Due to the handmade and often custom nature of our products, we generally do not accept returns. However, if there's an issue with your order, please contact us!

How long do custom orders take?

Custom order timelines vary depending on complexity and current workload, typically ranging from 2-4 weeks. We'll provide an estimated completion date with your quote.
