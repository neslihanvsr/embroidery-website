Custom Embroidery Orders

Have a unique idea you'd like to see brought to life in thread? HoyThreads specializes in creating personalized, one-of-a-kind embroidery pieces tailored to your vision. Whether it's a pet portrait, a special quote, or a design inspired by your favorite character, we're here to stitch your dreams into reality.

How It Works

1. Share Your Idea

Tell us about your concept, preferred colors, size, and any reference images you have. The more details, the better!

2. Get a Quote & Design

We'll provide a personalized quote and work with you on a preliminary design sketch. Your feedback is crucial!

3. Stitching Begins

Once approved, we'll begin the meticulous hand-stitching process, keeping you updated on progress.

4. Delivery

Your unique, handcrafted embroidery piece will be carefully packaged and shipped to you.
